The ladder
The same book, at the level you can read

Almonium adapts real novels to B1 and B2. Same plot, same voice, shorter sentences. Start where reading feels possible, step up when it feels easy, and finish with the author's own words.

B1 Adapted edition

Eines Morgens wachte Gregor Samsa aus unruhigen Träumen auf. Er lag in seinem Bett. Sein Körper war jetzt der Körper eines riesigen Insekts.

B2 Adapted edition

Als Gregor Samsa eines Morgens aus unruhigen Träumen erwachte, fand er sich in seinem Bett in ein riesiges Insekt verwandelt.

Original Kafka, 1915

Als Gregor Samsa eines Morgens aus unruhigen Träumen erwachte, fand er sich in seinem Bett zu einem ungeheueren Ungeziefer verwandelt.

Die Verwandlung, first sentence. Adapted editions are machine-assisted, reviewed, and labelled in the reader. Preview any of them free; the full ladder comes with Premium.

Review
Most apps say “wrong.” Almonium says which word you were thinking of.

If you answer with the meaning of a different word you are learning, Almonium recognises it, shows you both, and schedules the two together until you stop mixing them up.

It also rewords the answer every time, so you learn the word instead of memorising the shape of the card.
