Discover is the front door, the dictionary, and the only page a stranger sees first

One surface does three jobs: it is the public no-signup demo the brief calls the conversion mechanic, it is the word sheet the reader opens mid-paragraph, and it is where a word gets kept. Everything downstream — intent, confusion edges, encounter history — is decided here, at the moment of saving. The sheet is the same object for all three audiences; what changes is how much of it is filled in.

Two additions to what exists today: roots, because German confusion is morphological — the reason Ausgabe and Aufgabe cross is visible in their parts — and frequency with its provenance, replacing a bare score out of a hundred. Both are cached, deterministic, and free to serve.

1e Unfold and fold, live — the two motions Discover owns

The constitution gives the mark two meanings and forbids mixing them. Discover is the only surface that uses both, seconds apart: the entry unfolds while it loads, and folds when the word is kept.

Unfold One becoming many. Plays while the entry loads and while a word opens into its senses.
Fold Many becoming one. Plays once on Keep, inside the button, and does not repeat.
Unfold is the only loader on this route. Three to four blades inside 250ms, the rest blooming after, then a hold. If the entry is cached — which is the normal case — it never appears at all, and nothing flashes.
Fold belongs to Keep, not to the page. It runs inside the button at 26px, the label changes to Kept, and the sheet stays where it is. Saving must not navigate.
Neither is a celebration. Keeping a word happens forty times a session. No confetti, no particles, no sound — the three rationed celebrations are elsewhere.
Reduced motion. Unfold becomes a static mark at rest; fold becomes the label change alone.

The signed-out sheet is the marketing. It is a real page at a real URL, server-rendered, one per word, linked from the parallel-text pages. Cutting it down to tease a signup would cost the only acquisition channel that scales without the founder.
The wall is keeping, not knowing. Signed out, everything on the sheet is legible and nothing is blurred; the single ask sits at the bottom, where a stranger has already got what they came for.
The two member lines are the two that cost per use. Audio is spoken tokens; a fresh example is generated tokens. Everything else on the sheet was written once and is served from cache, so charging for it would be charging for nothing.
State the cap, not the restriction. At the hundredth word the sheet still opens and still says everything; Keep becomes a line reading “Free covers a hundred words — you have kept a hundred” with a plum link. Grey text, no raspberry.
What this asserts, and why
Frequency is evidence, not a score. A number out of a hundred with a mood attached — sixty, comfort zone — cannot be checked and does not say what it measures. A band with the count behind it can: how often, in what corpus, and whether that is print or speech. The same field also drives which words the reader is shown from a chapter.
The band names describe register, not the reader. “Common in print, uncommon in speech” is a fact about German. “You probably know it” is a guess about a person, and it will be wrong for the C1 reader who has never met the word, which is exactly the reader who just looked it up.
Roots carry the confusion. The reason Ausgabe and Aufgabe cross is that they differ by one prefix on one stem. Showing the family at lookup time is prevention; the confusion feedback in review is the cure. They are the same idea at two moments, and both fall out of one morphology field.
Etymology stops at the useful depth. Prefix, stem, and the living family a learner will actually meet. Proto-Germanic *gebaną is charming and does not help anyone hold two nouns apart; it belongs behind a link, if anywhere.
Intent is chosen at save, on this page. It is one tap with a sensible default, and it is the only moment the learner has the context to answer. Asking later, in review, is asking someone to remember why they saved something.
Senses are saved individually. The domain model has one spelling and many senses at different levels, so Keep binds to a sense, not to a spelling. Keeping Ausgabe as an edition and later as an expense is two items sharing one entry, not a duplicate.
The pasted sentence chooses the sense. Which is the argument for pasting, and the reason the demo asks for a sentence rather than a word. It is also the cheapest disambiguation available — one deterministic pass over the entry, no model call.
The entry states its own provenance. Version, date, and whether a human has corrected it. Corrections are how the database becomes better than the model that seeded it, and nobody reports an error in a source that will not admit to having one.
“Looked up, not kept” is free retention. Two lookups of the same word is the strongest save signal in the product and costs one row. It is also the honest version of a nag: a list, not a prompt.
The diacritic helper stays. A Ukrainian keyboard has no umlaut, and a learner should not have to know that oe works. The held-key popup from the current build is right; the visible row beneath it is for people who never discover a held key.

What this needs that does not exist yet: the structured entry with a morphology field and a family edge, wordfreq loaded with band thresholds per language and its provenance stored beside the number, sense-level saving, the correction queue, and server-rendered public word pages. The family edge is the same table as ConfusionEdge with a different type, so it costs nothing extra.

Decisions I made for you — overrule freely
The whole sheet is public. The alternative — two senses free, the rest behind signup — converts better in the short run and kills the SEO argument for having the page at all.
Frequency bands are named for register rather than for the learner's likely knowledge, which drops the “you probably know it” line from the current build. If you want a personal reading, it should come from your own history, not from a corpus.
The word family is capped at six visible, ordered by frequency, with the rest behind a link. For a productive prefix like ver- the true family runs to hundreds.
Audio is a member line. It is the only workload in the brief that scales linearly per user, and a free tier that speaks every lookup is the one way this page can cost real money.
No mascot anywhere on this route. Discover holds content on every state including the empty one, where the field itself is the instruction.
Etymology beyond the living family is left out entirely rather than shown collapsed. If you want it, it is a link to a separate panel, not a section on this page.
