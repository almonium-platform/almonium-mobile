Almonium · social & chat review

The one segment nobody had looked at

Friends, requests, chats and notifications. The structure is right — the panel model, the request lifecycle, saved messages and the hidden-chats idea all work. What is wrong is almost entirely surface: a saturated wallpaper on the surface holding the most reading outside the reader, a cyan avatar, tooltips in the wrong ink, icon-only actions with no labels, and a dark mode that stops at the page ground. The bell in 1a is the best-made thing in here. It only needs a dark counterpart.

P0Breaks a law
The Saved Messages avatar is cyan. A blue gradient disc with a white bookmark, sitting in a rail of plum emblems. It is the brightest object in the chat list and the last cyan in the product.
The chat wallpaper is a saturated raspberry field. Roughly #B0637E in light, deep maroon in dark, behind continuous reading. Law 3: surfaces holding content are quiet. Ground and hairline, nothing else — 1c.
Tooltips are filled plum. "Your friends, requests, and more", "Switch to visible chats". The action list already carries the fix for the blue tooltip: ink ground, cream text, tail clear of the card below. Plum is worse than blue here, because plum means primary action.
Exclamation marks in every notification. "@wowsie wants to be friends with you!", "accepted your friendship request!", "Nothing here… yet!". Register rule: no exclamation marks, and this audience chose the product for not being cheerful at them.
The eye icon is state drawn as action. Open eye, closed eye, tooltip reading "Switch to visible chats" — the user cannot tell whether they are looking at hidden chats or about to. Labelled switch, or a Hidden tab.
P1Visible quality
Every row action is a bare icon. Add-person, X, chat bubble, bell — accept, decline, cancel, message and notify all rendered as unlabelled glyphs, and the same person-plus glyph means both "send request" and "accept request".
Four identical unread notifications from one user. Same request, four rows, three timestamps. Collapse by actor and event type, keep the newest time — 1b.
The social menu spends a full panel on four links. Four stacked pill cards, one of them in an unexplained grey. Search is not a sibling of Friends, Requests and Blocked — it is the action you take from Friends.
The search field spell-checks the username. Red squiggle under "kuzan". spellcheck="false" and autocapitalize="none" on every handle field.
"We notified user about your request." Missing article, and it reports the system's work rather than the user's. "Request sent to @kuzanorest."
Language chips in the profile card are a rainbow. AM pink, HY green, AZ violet, ET orange, ES yellow, ID blue, each with a CEFR badge in a second hue. The multicolour palette belongs to interests only. Learning data takes plum and mono.
Chat list selection is a heavy filled block. Near-black maroon behind the row, dimming its own text. Selected is a plum tint plus a 3px edge, at normal text weight.

Why it went wrong
The whole search panel keeps light-mode surfaces in dark — white rows floating on the dark app. The icon looked fine and the panel around it burned.
Where the panel did darken, the plum stroke ran at about 2:1 against it. Plum is a ground colour in dark, never an ink one.
The spinner track was a light grey that outshone its own moving head, so the loading phase read as a static ring rather than a rotation.
Timing, easing and the swing curve are untouched. Two hexes and a surface.
1b Notifications — the panel, and what I think of it
Opinion
It is a log, and it should be an inbox. Every row tells you something happened, then sends you elsewhere to act. A friend request has exactly two answers and both fit in the row.
Four rows for one event. Whatever fires them, the panel is where the user meets the bug. Collapse on actor plus type regardless.
The count pill does nothing. Four unread, four red dots, and a pink "4" beside the title saying it a third time.
"Mark all as read" outweighs the heading. Plum 600 against ink 600 at almost the same size. It is a maintenance action — make it a quiet link.
Titles wrap to two lines and then the body repeats the same fact. One line: actor, verb, object.
Nothing ever gets old. No visible read state, no day grouping, no end. Grey the read rows and cap the list with "Older".

Also in this panel
Empty Friends reads "You don't have any friends yet." It is a contentless surface, so it belongs to Almo: "Almo hasn't found anyone here yet", with the one Find friends button it already has.

Questions I could not answer from the screens
Are the four Almonium language rooms broadcast channels or open group chats? They are the only chats a new account has, all four are empty, and nothing on the row says which.
Should hidden chats be a switch or a tab? A switch reads as a filter, a tab as a place. Switch if hiding is rare, tab if it is a real folder.
Does anything other than friend requests produce a notification today? If not, the panel and the Requests tab are the same list twice.
Is the date on the profile popover a join date? Unlabelled and next to the handle, it reads as a birthday.
