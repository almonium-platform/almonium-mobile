Almonium · dark mode rulebook · v1

Dark is a second system, not a filter

Companion to the design constitution. The constitution's laws all hold in dark; what changes is which value plays which role. The current dark build fails because only the page ground was darkened — cards, inputs and text colours kept their light-mode values, so half the interface is white and the other half is unreadable.

Scope: almonium-fe. Dark is a token flip on [data-theme="dark"] at the root — never a per-component override, never a CSS filter.

The five dark laws

Derived from the constitution. Where a light-mode value is quoted below, dark's job is to keep its role, not its hex.

1

Nothing inverts literally. White cards do not become black cards and ink text does not become white text. Each token is re-derived for its new ground.

2

Elevation is lightness, not shadow. A raised surface is a lighter surface plus a hairline. Shadow is invisible on dark and must not be relied on to separate a card from its ground.

3

The brand moves up the ramp, it does not change hue. Three tints extend the ramp's light end. Plum stays plum; it just stops being the darkest thing on screen and becomes the lightest.

4

No pure black and no pure white. Grounds carry the ink's warmth; text tops out at a warm off-white. Full-contrast pairs vibrate on OLED and make long reading sessions worse, which is the one thing dark mode exists to fix.

5

Saturation comes down as lightness comes up. Every fill that was a mid-tone hue in light — language rails, interest pills, status — is re-mixed at lower chroma. A saturated fill on a dark ground glares.

0 · What is broken today

Six failures, all from the same cause: the theme changed the ground and nothing else. Each is a fix, not a redesign.

White cards on a dark page

Profile, plan, App settings and every onboarding modal still render #fff. They are the brightest object on screen and burn. Card surface is a token; it must move with the theme.

Headings invisible

"Read", "Continue reading", "My books", "Appearance", "Navbar" are drawn in plum or ink on a near-black ground — 1.4:1. Page titles take the primary text token, never a brand hex.

Light-mode form controls

The search bar, the two selects and the empty filter pills on Read are still white/cream. Native select needs an explicit dark scheme or the OS paints it light.

Disabled reads as missing

"Manage plan", "Clear offline books", "Refresh" and the language-row labels sit near the ground and vanish. Disabled on dark lifts the label to #6E637A minimum and keeps its fill.

Pills and toggles keep light fills

Interest pills stay pastel-on-white; toggle tracks stay pale grey, so off looks brighter than on. Both sets get a dark-ground variant at the same hue.

No boundary anywhere

Where a surface did darken, it darkened to nearly the ground value and the shadow does nothing. Cards need the surface step and the hairline; the constitution's "no inner shadows" rule stands.

Four steps, each a warm plum-grey carrying the ink's hue. Anything deeper than the ground is prohibited: the reader must never sit in a well.

Mapping. Light #F9F6F5 ground → #14111A. Light #fff card → #1E1A26. Light #F9F6F5 nested block inside a card → #272130. Nesting goes up, one step per level, three levels maximum.
Every card carries 1px solid #332C3C in place of its light-mode shadow. Radius, padding and the "every panel is a card" law are unchanged.
Popovers, dropdowns and modals sit at step 3 with the same hairline plus 0 16px 40px rgba(0,0,0,.55) — the one place shadow still earns its keep, as a halo separating the overlay from a scrim, not as a lift.
Scrim rgba(10,8,14,.72), no blur. Onboarding's particle field stays visible through it at reduced opacity.
The dark ink surface from light mode has no counterpart. Where light mode used #2C2530 as a contrast panel (email shell, constitution header, footers), dark uses the card step and a hairline instead. Two dark panels on a dark ground are one panel.

2 · Ink & brand tints

Text — four levels, warm, never #fff
#F1EAEF — headings, UI text (was #2C2530)
#BEB2C2 — subheads, secondary prose (was #5f5560)
#8D8298 — metadata, placeholder (was #a99aa8)

Reader body drops to 400 weight at 17–19px as in light mode, but the ground is #1E1A26, not the page ground — the page still holds a page.

The tint end — three new ramp steps, no new hues

Law 1 of the constitution permits extending the ramp's ends. Dark extends the light end, exactly as steps 7–8 extended the dark end for light mode. These are app-only and never appear in the mark.

The primary button inverts its relationship to the page. In light mode it is the darkest object and its label is cream. In dark mode it is the lightest object and its label is #14111A. Filling it with #5A1A74 on a dark ground makes the one committing action the least visible thing on screen.
Saved words, links, active nav, blade-gauge fill take #CBA3E0. Marginalia takes #C297D6 at 80%.
Nothing below #9059BC may carry text or meaning on a dark ground. Steps 1–8 of the original ramp survive only as fills behind cream text — the premium gradient, the emblem, the language rail.
Status — re-mixed, still off-ramp
#3DD69B success #FF8FA3 error #F0A184 warning #12332A success ground

Status text on a dark card uses the tint; status fills behind dark text use the same tint. The light-mode hexes are never drawn on dark — #fa4666 on #14111A is 3.1:1 and fails body text.

Prohibited

Pure black grounds and pure white text. filter: invert() or any global blend hack. Neutral greys with no hue — a cold grey card next to a plum-grey card reads as a bug. New hues invented for dark; extend the tint end instead. Light-mode brand hexes used as text. Elevation by shadow alone.

3 · Controls

Primary — #B07BD0 fill, #14111A label · hover #E0C4EE + translateY(-1px) · pressed #9059BC. Hover lightens in dark, where light mode darkened. Drop the coloured drop-shadow; it does nothing on dark.
Secondary — outline #6B6178 with primary-text label, filling to a 8% white state layer. It moves toward light, the mirror of light mode's move toward ink.
Disabled — background:#2A2433; color:#6E637A. Still one step above the card so the control's footprint survives; a disabled control that disappears is worse in dark than in light.
Inputs — fill #272130, border #453D51, text #F1EAEF, placeholder #8D8298, focus ring 2px #CBA3E0. Set color-scheme: dark on the root or native selects, checkboxes, scrollbars and autofill stay light.
Toggles — off track #453D51 with a #8D8298 knob; on track #9059BC with a #F1EAEF knob. On must always be brighter than off, which the current build inverts.
State layers — hover is white at 6%, pressed white at 10%, selected rgba(203,163,224,.14) with a #CBA3E0 label. Light mode's black-at-4% layers are invisible here.

4 · Brand assets, imagery, colour sets

Wordmark. wordmark-cream.svg replaces the plum wordmark everywhere in dark. Never recolour the plum file with a filter; ship the cream asset.
Emblem — size decides the variant, in both themes. The ten-tone ramp emblem needs ~64px to resolve; below that the tonal steps average into a purple smudge and the blade gaps close. The rule: ramp emblem at 64px and up, flat emblem below. Nav, favicons, avatars and list rows are always flat.
dark nav · #CBA3E0
light nav · #7D2060
#7D2060 on dark · 2.3:1
Flat fill is a token, not a constant. Light uses #7D2060; dark uses #CBA3E0. The plum flat mark on a dark ground is 2.3:1 and reads as a dark stain — the same failure as the plum headings. Ship the two files, don't filter.
No disc behind a small emblem. The white-disc seal is a large-format lockup; at nav size the disc becomes a bright dot with a smudge in it and it is the brightest object in a dark navbar. Place the flat mark directly on the bar.
Almo. Plum ink #612B5E disappears on dark. Dark mode uses a second engraving pass in #C79BD8 — same drawing, re-inked, exported as a paired asset. Hatched line art must never be inverted programmatically; the hatching goes muddy.
Book covers and imagery are content and keep their own values. A cream paper cover is correct on a dark shelf. Do not dim, do not tint; give each cover a 1px rgba(0,0,0,.35) inner edge so white covers do not bleed into the ground.
Language hues (the edge rail and code badges) drop to ~55% saturation and rise in lightness: the rail stays 3px, the badge text takes the hue's tint rather than the hue itself.
Interest pills keep their eight hues at 18% opacity over the card, with the label in the hue's tint. The pastel-on-white fills from light mode are not reused.
Particle field — particles drop to rgba(232,220,228,.18). The current dark auth screen is close to right; it is the only screen that is.

6 · Login

The auth screen is the one place dark is nearly right today, and the one place where a wrong provider treatment does the most damage — it is the first surface a new user sees.

Not brand-filled discs

Three saturated circles become the brightest, loudest objects on a near-black screen, and the white Apple disc glares hardest of all. They out-shout the wordmark and the greeting.

Not fully monochrome

Three identical grey chips with faint glyphs read as one control group, not three choices, and lose the colour recognition that makes a provider button instant. Apple's guidelines also require its mark be full black or full white.

Brand mark on a neutral chip

The chip is a surface (step 2 + hairline) so it obeys the elevation rule; the mark inside keeps its own colours, so it stays recognisable without lighting up the page. Provider logos are content, like book covers.

The card is a surface, not a void. #1E1A26 with the #332C3C hairline and the overlay halo. The current card is darker than the page it sits on, which inverts the elevation rule — the one object that should feel lifted is the one sunk furthest.
Label the buttons. Bare discs make the user decode three glyphs; on the widest screen there is room for a word next to each mark. If the layout must stay compact, keep circular chips at the same fill and border and add a visible label underneath — never nothing.
Chip spec — fill #272130, border #453D51, radius 12, min height 44, hover white-at-6%, focus ring 2px #CBA3E0. Identical geometry for all three — differences between providers live in the mark alone.
Emblem above the wordmark, not behind it. Today it straddles the card's top-right corner and is clipped by it, at a size where the ramp is already unreadable. Flat tint mark, 32–36px, centred over the wordmark.
Particles take the tint at low alpha — rgba(203,163,224,.13) and a soft shape. Hard grey rectangles read as broken image tiles rather than atmosphere, and they should never approach the card's own value.
The greeting is the localisation cue and stays in Literata at secondary text; the wordmark stays primary. Terms and privacy links take #CBA3E0 at 11.5px — they are absent from the current screen.

One flip, one place. If a component needs a [data-theme="dark"] .thing rule, the thing is hardcoding a hex. Fix the component, not the theme.
Theme setting is Light / Dark / System, resolved before first paint from a tiny inline script — a light flash on a dark theme is the most-reported dark-mode bug there is.
Ship checks: no #fff, #000 or literal ramp hex outside the token file · every screen at 4.5:1 body / 3:1 UI · every native control and scrollbar dark · email dark-mode inversion tested in Gmail iOS and Outlook · screenshots of both themes side by side before merge.
Order of repair, highest damage first: card surface token → text tokens → form controls and selects → toggles and pills → disabled states → Almo and wordmark assets.
